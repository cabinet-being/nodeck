Save to nodeck — settings

How to use

Click the nodeck button in the toolbar: a panel lists the images and videos of the page. Choose the ones you want, put them in order and save them as separate cards (the default), a comic or a set. Tags, the source site and the page link are filled in; edit them before saving.

Right-click an image or video → Save to nodeck… to save just that one. Saving continues when the panel closes; open it again on the same tab to see the progress.
